What I do

Six tractor jobs.

Rough drives, grown-up fields, fence lines, plots, and ditches. I show up with the Kioti and the attachment the job actually needs, and I finish it the way I would on my own ground.

01

Brush hogging

Rotary cutter for fields, trails, fence lines, and acreage that has grown up. Tall weeds, briars, and thick cover come down so the ground is usable again.

02

Dirt grading & fill work

Box blade and loader work to cut, fill, level, and shape dirt. Drive lanes, pads, and rough ground get brought to the grade the site needs.

03

Gravel driveways

Install, regrade, crown, and repair potholes and washouts. Gravel goes down and gets bladed so water sheds off the drive, including 21AA limestone.

04

Tilling gardens & food plots

Tiller work for gardens and food plots. I break new ground or work up a plot so it is ready to plant.

05

Brush cleanup & land / fence-line clearing

Loader and stump bucket for brush, fence lines, and debris. Tractor-scale clearing on overgrown edges, trails, and acreage.

06

Ditch work & drainage

I shape, clean, and pull ditches so water leaves the drive and the field instead of cutting ruts through the work.
